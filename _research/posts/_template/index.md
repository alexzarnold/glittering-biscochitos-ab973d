## The takeaway

Lead with the conclusion in two or three sentences. A reader who stops here should still leave with the point.

## Setup

## Math

Inline math goes between single dollar signs, like $\Delta = \partial V / \partial S$.

Display math goes between double dollar signs on their own lines:
