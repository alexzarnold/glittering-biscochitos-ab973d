What we do

Four things.

You do not need to know any of this yet. That is what the meetings are for.

01

Think in odds

Nobody knows what a stock will do next. So we talk in odds instead of predictions.

02

Ask what it cost

Doubling your money is only impressive once you know what you put at risk to do it.

03

Argue properly

Somebody makes a case. The room tries to break it. What survives is worth keeping.

04

Get ready for the job

This way of thinking is what trading and finance interviews actually test.

The idea in one picture

Most finance clubs pick stocks. We look at the shape of the bet.

Here is the kind of thing we spend a meeting on. An option is a contract. You pay a small amount today for the right to buy something later at a price you lock in now. If it never gets there, you walk away and lose what you paid. Nothing more.

That is the chart. Flat on the left: the price stayed low, so you lost what you paid and only that. Rising on the right: the price went up, and there is no ceiling on the line.

Small known loss, large unknown gain. Once you can see that shape, you start asking better questions about every bet you take, in markets and out of them.
