Legal

The necessary small print.

Short, and worth reading once.

We are not the university

ORCA is a student organization, not a department.

ORCA is a registered student organization at the University of Colorado Boulder. It is not a department, office, or agent of the university, and it does not act on the university's behalf.

Nothing on this site is an official university communication. The university does not endorse ORCA's purpose or opinions and is not responsible for anything published here. ORCA uses no University of Colorado logos or marks without written approval; the ORCA wordmark and fin are the club's own.

This site is not hosted on university systems and is maintained by the ORCA Executive Board.

Not investment advice

We teach. We do not advise, and we do not manage money.

ORCA is an educational organization. Nothing on this site, and nothing said at a meeting, is investment, legal, or tax advice, or an offer or solicitation to buy or sell any security.

ORCA does not manage money. The club does not pool member funds, run a fund, hold a trading account, or invest capital on anyone's behalf. Nobody here will ask you for money to invest. No officer or member acts as a registered investment adviser or broker-dealer for the club.

Anything you do with your own money is your decision and your responsibility. Derivatives carry real risk of total loss. An option can and often does expire worthless.

ORCA makes no claims about past or future investment returns, its own or anyone else's.

Your information

What we collect and what we do with it.

The sign-up form asks for your name, CU email, year, major, market experience, and what you are interested in.

We use it for one thing: telling you when and where meetings are.

  • Responses are processed by Formspree, a third-party form service, and reach the Executive Board by email.
  • We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone outside the club.
  • Every email we send has an unsubscribe link.
  • Email orca@colorado.edu to have your details deleted and to come off the list. No reason needed, and we will do it promptly.
  • This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and tracks nothing.

Do not send us government ID numbers, student ID numbers, financial account details, or passwords. We do not need them and will not ask for them.

Accessibility

Built to WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

Real heading structure, visible keyboard focus, alt text on every image, color contrast checked against the standard, and reduced-motion support throughout.

If any part of this site is hard to use, email orca@colorado.edu and tell us what got in the way. We will fix it and send you whatever you were trying to reach.
