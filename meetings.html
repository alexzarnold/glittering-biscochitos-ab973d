Before you come

What a first meeting is like

  • Can I just show up? Yes. No sign-up, no list at the door.
  • How long? About an hour.
  • Do I prepare anything? No.
  • What happens? Half a topic walkthrough, half discussion and worked examples.
  • Will I be called on? Not unless you want to be.
  • Never invested anything? That is the most common starting point in the room.
